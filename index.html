Right now, the story of your home lives in a kitchen drawer, three inboxes and your memory. Henry Haus gives it one place to live.

What Henry does for you

Three jobs, off your plate.

Everything, on file

Appliances, model numbers, manuals, warranties and receipts, organized room by room. When something breaks, the answer is two taps away instead of a drawer away.

Maintenance that comes to you

A care schedule built from what’s actually in your home. Henry reminds you before the filter clogs or the season turns, so small jobs never become expensive ones.

Know what you’re covered for

See which warranties are live, when they end, and what your home insurance does and doesn’t cover — in plain language, before you need to file a claim.

One home or several.

Every house gets its own file, with its own records, reminders and coverage. Switch between them in a tap.

Vacation homes

Seasonal reminders for opening and closing the house, every appliance and repair on file, and no more guessing what’s there when you’re hundreds of miles away. Works whether you rent it out or keep it for yourself.

A parent’s or relative’s home

Keep track of the furnace, the warranties and the insurance for someone you love, without digging through their drawers. When something needs doing, you’ll know before they have to call you.

Rental properties

One file per property, with appliances, service history, warranties and coverage. Plan maintenance between tenants, and hand a contractor everything they need in one link.

How it works
  1. 01

    Walk your home

    Snap the label on each appliance and forward the receipts you already have. No spreadsheets, no typing model numbers.

  2. 02

    Henry organizes it

    Henry finds the manuals, dates the warranties and files everything where you’d expect to look.

  3. 03

    Stay a step ahead

    Timely reminders, warranty alerts and coverage check-ins arrive on their own. You just get on with your week.
